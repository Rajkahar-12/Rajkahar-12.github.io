PROJECT 02 / AI & EDUCATION

RashAI.

An AI-powered study companion designed to make learning more accessible, organized and interactive.

TYPE AI Study Companion
TECHNOLOGIES HTML · CSS · JavaScript
STATUS Live

01 / THE IDEA

Learning should feel
less complicated.

RashAI is an AI-powered study companion created to support students in their learning journey.

The goal is to bring study assistance and useful learning tools together in one accessible and easy-to-use interface.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Built around
the learner.

The interface focuses on a comfortable study experience, with a clean layout and interactive tools designed for everyday learning.

I wanted to explore how AI and web technologies could be combined to create something genuinely useful for students.

03 / THE BUILD

Exploring the
possibilities of AI.

Building RashAI gave me an opportunity to experiment with frontend development, interactive interfaces and AI integration.

It is an ongoing learning experience that continues to shape how I approach building useful digital products.
