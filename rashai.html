Behind the project

01 — OVERVIEW

Making AI feel like a helpful study partner.

RashAI is an AI-powered study companion created to help students explore topics, understand concepts and make their learning experience more interactive.

The interface focuses on a clean study environment with features such as mathematical formatting, voice interaction and file support.
