ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making learning more accessible.

RashAI is a study companion concept built around the idea of making technology useful for students.

The application focuses on an easy-to-use interface and AI-powered learning assistance. It is designed to provide students with a more convenient way to interact with their study material.

Building RashAI helped me explore frontend development, responsive design, JavaScript interactions and integrating AI-powered functionality into a web application.
