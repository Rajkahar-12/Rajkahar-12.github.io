Behind the project

01 — OVERVIEW

From a simple idea to a complete presentation.

SlideWala is an AI-powered web application designed to help users generate presentation content from a topic.

Users can customize their presentation based on slide count, audience, tone and visual theme. The generated content can then be exported as a PowerPoint file.
